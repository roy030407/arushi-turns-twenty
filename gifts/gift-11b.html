DIL KA NAUTANKI
a film about being obvious and getting away with it
Dost the. Phir kya hua? Wahi hua jo hona tha.
STARRING
Arushi Pundir ... as Herself (in all your complexity)
Roy ... as The One Who Noticed First
The Group Chat ... as Greek Chorus
That One Exam Season ... as The Antagonist
Distance ... as Plot Device
ACT ONE: THE MEET

The setting: unremarkable. Two people in the same place at the same time, as people often are. No significant eye contact. No dramatic music. No slow-motion hair flip. Just: oh, this person exists. Noted. Filed. Moved on.

Except not moved on. Because here is the thing about you - you are the kind of person someone notices and then cannot un-notice. Not because you are trying to be noticed. Because you are simply being yourself, which turns out to be a lot.

ACT TWO: SIRF DOST

The declaration: 'We are just friends.' Repeated with increasing conviction to decreasing audiences. The group chat knows. The timing of the texts knows. The way he saves your name in his phone knows.

You, for your part, are entirely plausible as Just Friends. This is the most unnerving part. You are good at things. You are good at this. You say 'yaar' with exactly the correct frequency and laugh at the appropriate moments and genuinely seem to mean it.

Reader: you do not mean it in the way you say it. Neither does he. Both of you know. Both of you continue.

INTERVAL
snacks are available in the lobby. the characters are also taking a break to figure things out.
ACT THREE: CLARITY (FINALLY)

There is a moment - not dramatic, not orchestrated - when the obvious becomes something you say out loud instead of just knowing.

It is not a grand gesture. It is not a montage. It is just: finally. Finally the word catches up with the feeling. Finally the conversation is the conversation it has been trying to be.

You look exactly the way you always have. That is, of course, the entire point.

THE OPEN ENDING

This film does not end. It is still in production. The leads are still in it. The story is still being written.

Rating: inevitable. Running time: ongoing. Sequels: expected.
