not because you are them. because they remind me of you.

🎬 Geet Jab We Met
Geet
Jab We Met, 2007
You walk into a room and the room rearranges itself around you. Not because you demand it - because you simply take up exactly as much space as you were supposed to. You are loud in the best possible way, certain about what you want, and entirely unbothered by what you are not. You have a plan and a backup plan and a feeling about the backup plan. You turn strangers into people you know. You make the journey the destination. Geet is chaos with a compass. So are you.
🎬 Andie Anderson How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days
Andie Anderson
How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days, 2003
You are the most put-together person in the room and you know it - with a lightness that makes everyone adore you for it rather than resent it. You can walk into any situation and make it yours without making it feel like a takeover. You are funny when you choose to be and sharp when you need to be. You dress well because you respect yourself. You win arguments without raising your voice. You do things with style not because style is the point, but because you simply can't do them any other way.
🎬 Rachel Green Friends
Rachel Green
Friends, 1994–2004
You figure yourself out. That is the whole arc, and it is a beautiful one. You start knowing your taste and learn to trust it. You build your own version of a life - not the version handed to you, not the version expected of you, but the one you actually wanted. You are surrounded by people who love you and you love them back fiercely, imperfectly, completely. You get better at knowing what you want without losing any of what makes you yourself.
🎬 Donna Paulsen Suits
Donna Paulsen
Suits, 2011–2019
You are the person in the room who has read the situation three steps ahead of everyone else - and are choosing, generously, not to say so yet. You are loyal in a way that is almost architectural. You will walk through a difficult moment without needing applause afterward. You have standards, and you don't apologise for them. You are elegant, you are capable, you are funny in a way that cuts through everything - and you know exactly when to use which version of yourself.
🎬 Imara Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara
Imara
Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara, 2011
You turn up in someone's life and somehow that person starts remembering what they actually want. Not because you tell them - because of how you are. You are present. You are certain. You make being alive look like something worth being careful with. You don't fix anyone. You just remind them, quietly, that there's more to them than the version they've been performing.
