⚖ COURT SEAL
IN THE MATTER OF: THE HEART v. ROY
Case No. 002006 · Docket: You Know What You Did
PREAMBLE

This matter comes before the Court on the petition of The Heart, seeking formal acknowledgment of the following: that one Roy, hereinafter 'the Defendant,' has conducted himself in a manner that - while technically defensible - has resulted in a series of events the Defendant was fully aware would result in exactly this outcome.

The Heart files this action not out of complaint, but out of an obligation to the record.

EXHIBIT A: THE ORDINARY THINGS HE NOTICED

The Defendant noticed the way you laugh at your own jokes. The particular sound of your voice when you're excited about something you want to explain. The face you make when you're pretending something isn't funny. The way you enter a room. The way you talk when you forget to edit yourself. The way you exist, generally.

The Court notes that noticing these things is not, in itself, actionable. The Court also notes that the Defendant noticed all of them and filed them carefully, which suggests intent.

EXHIBIT B: THE RECORD OF PATIENCE

The Defendant waited. He waited through Sirf Dost. He waited through 'we're just friends' said with decreasing conviction. He waited through every group call, every late text, every exam season, every distance.

The Heart submits that this kind of patience is either extremely disciplined or completely inevitable - and that in this case, it was both.

EXHIBIT C: THE ONGOING OFFENCE

The Defendant continues, as of the date of this filing, to be exactly himself. He has made no attempt to be less. The Heart finds this unreasonable and fully intends to continue finding it so.

GUILTY
Lucky. Indefinitely.
The Heart rests. The matter is ongoing. Court adjourned.
The Heart
