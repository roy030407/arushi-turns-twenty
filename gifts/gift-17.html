Monday, 12 June 2006 · The 163rd Day of the Year · Doha, Qatar

THE WORLD ARRANGED ITSELF,
AND SHE ARRIVED.

12 June 2006 · The 163rd Day of the Year · Summer Had Just Begun

NOW PLAYING IN CINEMAS

On the day Arushi Pundir arrived in the world, audiences across India were watching Fanaa — a film about love, loss, and a woman who is, by all accounts, more interesting than anyone around her. In Hollywood, Pixar's Cars had just opened, and the world was driving somewhere, though it didn't quite know where yet.

It was a summer of stories. A reasonable one to be born into.

THE NUMBER ONE SONG

Shakira's 'Hips Don't Lie,' featuring Wyclef Jean, held the number one position globally on 12 June 2006. This is the song that was playing somewhere in the world the moment she arrived. The Court has noted this as evidence.

"She was born the same day Shakira told the world hips don't lie. The timing is noted."

SPORTS DESK: THE WORLD CUP

The 2006 FIFA World Cup was underway in Germany. On June 12, the tournament was in its group stage — Italy, France, Brazil, Germany all still in it. The world was watching football. It had other things to attend to, as it turned out.

THE YEAR SHE ARRIVED IN

2006 was the year YouTube was a new thing. The year Twitter was about to exist. The year of the iPod nano and flip phones and ringtones you downloaded one at a time.

She arrived into a world that was just learning what a smartphone would be — a world that would change completely before she was old enough to drive. She handled it.

THE WORLD'S VERDICT

Twelve years from 2006, she would be in Doha. Six years after that, she would be twenty. The days in between: 7,305 of them. Each one hers. None of them wasted.

She was born on 12 June 2006. The rest of us just got luckier.

"She was born on 12 June 2006. The rest of us just got luckier."
